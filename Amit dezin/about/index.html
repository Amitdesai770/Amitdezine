Hi, I'm Amit. I blend my science roots with product design to make interfaces that just… make sense.

View Work

Before I knew what a wireframe was, I already had two obsessions that shouldn’t have belonged together: taking things apart to understand them, and putting color on paper until something looked right. One was science. The other was painting. Nobody told me they’d end up being the same skill.

Product design didn’t feel like a career change when I found it — it felt like the room where both of those obsessions had been waiting for me the whole time. Observation from one. Composition from the other. Design just gave them a shared job.

I’ve since spent my time building AI-native, shipping real interfaces, and getting genuinely obsessed with the difference between a screen that works and one that belongs. During internships at HDFC Bank and Virgio, that obsession got tested against the real thing — a commercial cards portal redesigned around the one gap every competitor missed, and a checkout flow rebuilt off Shopify that never looked back.

If you’ve ever pulled something apart just to see how it worked — we’ll get along.

Lets create
incredible work together.
